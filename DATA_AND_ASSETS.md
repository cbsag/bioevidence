# Data and asset provenance

The Apache-2.0 license in `LICENSE` applies to BioEvidence's original source
code and documentation. It does **not** grant new rights to third-party
datasets, article content, externally supplied model weights, indexes, or
third-party imagery.

- `data/shared-task/topics.json` adapts the public [TREC 2024 BioGen topics](https://trec.nist.gov/data/biogen2024.html). The questions and narratives originate with the track organizers and retain their original terms. They are included for research demonstration, not relicensed under Apache-2.0.
- `data/shared-task/system-*.jsonl` contains archived BioEvidence research-run outputs. These are examples, not newly generated answers or claims of clinical validity.
- `public/official-results-page.png` depicts official TREC results and is included for context. NIST/TREC names and marks remain with their owners.
- `public/ganesh-chandrasekar.jpg` is a personal photograph; it is not licensed for reuse by this repository's code license.
- `public/bioevidence/*` contains BioEvidence visual identity assets. The code license does not grant trademark or brand-use rights beyond the terms of Apache-2.0 section 6.
- PubMed abstracts, ClinicalTrials.gov records, Europe PMC records, model weights, and retrieval indexes are fetched or installed separately. Check each provider's terms before redistribution.

If you are building a derivative project, replace the archived examples and
personal/brand assets with material you are entitled to distribute. Please
report any provenance concern in an issue so it can be reviewed promptly.
